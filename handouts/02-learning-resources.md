**Simulation for Robotics — SCAD · Savannah College of Art & Design — D. Pillis, Robotics Professor Demo**

Links checked October 2026. ★ = good first step.

### Start today

- ★ **This repository** — every demo runs on a laptop: `pip install -r requirements-demo.txt`, then `python demo/02_drive_the_robot.py`.
- ★ **MuJoCo tutorial notebook** (runs in the browser on Google Colab) — <https://colab.research.google.com/github/google-deepmind/mujoco/blob/main/python/tutorial.ipynb>
- **MuJoCo Playground notebooks** — GPU robot learning in Colab: locomotion, manipulation, vision — <https://playground.mujoco.org>
- **mjlab demo, no install** — `uvx --from mjlab --refresh demo` — <https://github.com/mujocolab/mjlab>
- **ArtifactArena Play** — design a robot as MJCF + Python and test it in the browser — <https://artifactarena.ai/play>

### Books (several free online)

- ★ Kevin Lynch and Frank Park, *Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control* (Cambridge, 2017). Free preprint and videos: <https://hades.mech.northwestern.edu/index.php/Modern_Robotics>
- Roland Siegwart, Illah Nourbakhsh and Davide Scaramuzza, *Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots*, 2nd ed. (MIT Press, 2011). Mobile robots, sensors, mapping — the topics of Demos 2–4.
- Sebastian Thrun, Wolfram Burgard and Dieter Fox, *Probabilistic Robotics* (MIT Press, 2005). The reference for mapping, localisation and SLAM.
- Steven LaValle, *Planning Algorithms* (Cambridge, 2006). Free: <http://lavalle.pl/planning/>
- Russ Tedrake, *Underactuated Robotics* and *Robotic Manipulation* (MIT course notes, free, with runnable notebooks): <https://underactuated.mit.edu> · <https://manipulation.mit.edu>
- Kevin Lynch, Nicholas Marchuk and Matthew Elwin, *Embedded Computing and Mechatronics with the PIC32 Microcontroller* (Newnes, 2015), for students moving from simulation to their own hardware.

### Video courses and lectures

- *Modern Robotics* specialization (Northwestern, Coursera) — video lectures that accompany the book; linked from the book page above.
- Cyrill Stachniss, lectures on mobile robotics, SLAM and photogrammetry (University of Bonn), free on YouTube — search "Cyrill Stachniss SLAM course".
- MIT *Robotic Manipulation* and *Underactuated Robotics* lecture videos (Russ Tedrake) — linked from the course sites above.
- NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute — free self-paced Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab courses: <https://learn.nvidia.com>
